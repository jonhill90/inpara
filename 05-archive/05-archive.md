# Archive

**Purpose**: things that arrive already finished or replaced and are kept only as a record, such as an old snapshot (wizard step 2 in [ORGANIZE.md](../99-meta/ORGANIZE.md)). Each record is one folder, `05-archive/<name>/`, kept unchanged. Lettered sub-kind folders are added here only by a new version of the standard.

## What does not go here

Finished work does not move here:

- **A finished project, note or map stays where it is.** Set `status: replaced` in its front matter, and say in the item what replaced it. Moving it would break every link to it.
- **Git history is the archive** of every earlier version.
- **To hide finished work** from a map, move its link to a "Finished" section of that map instead of moving the file.
