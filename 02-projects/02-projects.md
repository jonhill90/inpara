# Projects

**Purpose**: the plan, status and log of work that will end (wizard step 4 in [ORGANIZE.md](../99-meta/ORGANIZE.md)).

## Shape

```text
02-projects/
└── <project>.md     the map note: objective, status, and links (each with a reason) to what the project uses
```

- The map note is to a project what an area map is to an area: links only, and no folder. Start from [project-map.md](../99-meta/templates/project-map.md).
- A project's pages are notes of the kind they are: a plan or status page kept current in `01-notes/01h-handbook/`, a note on one idea in `01-notes/01a-atomic/`, a task in `01-notes/01t-tasks/`, and a finished review kept only as a record in `05-archive/`. The map links each one.
- **When the project ends**, it stays where it is: set `status: replaced` in its front matter and write the outcome in the map. Links keep working, and git history keeps every version.

## What makes a project

- **Clear objective.** A specific outcome you're working toward.
- **Actionable tasks.** Concrete steps you can take.
- **Defined scope.** A beginning, middle and end.
- **Knowledge foundation.** Built on notes and resources, linked from the map.

Projects emerge when areas and notes reveal a specific problem, an opportunity, a skills gap or an idea ready to build. Areas are where you notice them: an area map's "Potential projects" section is the usual source.

## Status

`draft` while planning, `current` while active or on hold (say which in the map), `replaced` when done or cancelled.
